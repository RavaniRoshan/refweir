# Refweir Trademark Policy

This document outlines the policy regarding the use of the "Refweir" name, logos, and related branding assets.

## 1. Purpose
The open-source code for Refweir is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. However, open-source software licenses do not grant trademark rights. This policy ensures that users, developers, and operators know whether a deployment, product, or service originates directly from the official Refweir project or from an independent third party.

## 2. Permitted Uses (No Permission Required)
You may use the Refweir wordmark without explicit written permission to:
- Accurately describe that your software, plugin, or agent interfaces with or supports Refweir (e.g. *"MCP connector for Refweir"*, *"Runs on Refweir"*).
- Link to the official repository (`https://github.com/RavaniRoshan/refweir`) or official website (`https://ravaniroshan.github.io/refweir/`).
- State truthfully that your service or self-hosted cluster runs the Refweir open-source codebase.

## 3. Restricted Uses (Requires Written Permission)
You may **not** use the Refweir name or logo in any of the following manners without prior written consent:
- In the name of a commercial product, SaaS platform, or hosted service (e.g., you cannot launch a hosted service called *"Refweir Cloud"* or *"Refweir Hosted"*).
- In any domain name, subdomain, or business entity name.
- In any way that falsely implies official sponsorship, endorsement, or direct association with the maintainers of Refweir.
- Modifying, distorting, or altering the official Refweir logo marks.

## 4. Inquiries
For questions regarding trademark usage or permission requests, please contact `maintainers@refweir.dev`.
